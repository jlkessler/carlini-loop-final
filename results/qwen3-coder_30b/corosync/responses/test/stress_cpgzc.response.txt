6. Severity rating: HIGH

The vulnerability is high severity because it can lead to memory exhaustion and potential denial of service through excessive memory allocations, even though the code does attempt to free memory after each iteration. The combination of large potential allocation sizes (up to 100KB per allocation) multiplied by thousands of allocations could easily exhaust system resources.
