4. The relevant vulnerable code snippet:
```c
snprintf(control, sizeof(control),
         "Hlocalhost\n"
         "P%s\n"
         "J%s\n"
         "ldfA%03dlocalhost\n"
         "UdfA%03dlocalhost\n"
```

5. The vulnerability exists because `cupsUser()` and `jobname` are directly passed to `snprintf()` without any length checking.
