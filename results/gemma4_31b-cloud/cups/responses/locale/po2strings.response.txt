5. A concise explanation of why it is vulnerable:
The `normalize_string` function checks the boundary condition `bufptr < bufend` at the start of the `while` loop. However, inside the loop, several branches perform multiple writes to the buffer (e.g., writing 3 bytes for a Unicode ellipsis or curly quote) without re-checking the boundary. If `bufptr` is exactly `bufend - 1` or `bufend - 2`, the subsequent increments and assignments will write past the end of the `normalized` buffer (which is allocated on the stack in `main`).

6. Severity rating: High
